Compute and storage, made simple

Carolina Cloud is built for heavy scientific computing. Most of our customers run bioinformatics pipelines, quantitative finance models, and other data-intensive workloads, and our hardware and instance design reflect that focus. We build nodes with AMD EPYC silicon and large amounts of RAM, and we preinstall the tooling needed for jobs that hammer cores, memory, and disk for hours at a time. The same plain Ubuntu VMs also work nicely for lighter loads like web hosting and small services.

Prefer the interactive version? Open it here.

Compute: dedicated cores, pre-built environments

Every compute product runs on dedicated AMD EPYC cores. Pick a clean VM or container, or start from a ready-to-use notebook or IDE. Every instance ships with a fully persistent environment and disk that survive stops and restarts, backed by fast NVMe storage with high read and write throughput. All containers also come with mounted S3-compatible object storage buckets, live resizing of resources up or down, and live usage monitoring in the console. GPU instances with NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell ($1.75/hr) and RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell are also available.

Nextflow: our own executor

nf-ccloud runs your pipelines on a warm pool of workers that get resized between tasks instead of destroyed and rebuilt. Workers are sized to each process's own directives, cost is visible per task while the run is going, and the plugin is in the official Nextflow registry, so it drives runs from a head container on our console or from your own laptop, HPC login node, or CI runner.
