Carolina Cloud pricing

How much does Carolina Cloud cost?

$0.015 per vCPU-hour including 2 GiB of RAM, on dedicated AMD EPYC cores, with $0 egress. That is roughly a third of the on-demand price of the same instance shape on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.

One published rate card, with no egress charges and no hidden line items. Every vCPU is dedicated, never shared, so the price you see is the performance you get.

Pricing questions

How much does a VM cost on Carolina Cloud?

$0.015 per vCPU-hour including 2 GiB of RAM on general-purpose cores ($0.005/vCPU/hr plus $0.005/GiB RAM/hr), or $0.02 per vCPU-hour on high-performance cores. An 8 vCPU / 16 GiB instance is $0.12/hr (about $88/month). NVMe disk is $0.0001/GiB/hr.

Does Carolina Cloud charge egress fees?

No. Data transfer out is $0, with no allowance, caps or metering.

How much does a GPU cost on Carolina Cloud?

An NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell is $1.75/hr. RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell GPUs are also available. Carolina Cloud does not offer H100, H200 or B200 GPUs.

How much is object storage?

Warm S3-compatible storage is $0.009/GiB/month and cold storage is $1.55/TB/month, both with $0 egress. boto3, rclone and the AWS CLI work unchanged.

Is there a commitment or minimum spend?

No. Carolina Cloud prices are flat list rates with no commitment, no egress charges and no minimum spend. Stopping an instance pauses compute billing and keeps your data.

Are there free credits?

North Carolina startups can get $250 in free credits, and we offer migration credits for teams moving off AWS. Email hello@carolinacloud.io.
