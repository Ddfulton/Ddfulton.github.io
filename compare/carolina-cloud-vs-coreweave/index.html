Carolina Cloud vs CoreWeave

Should you use Carolina Cloud or CoreWeave?

If you're an AI lab or enterprise training on hundreds or thousands of GPUs, CoreWeave is built for you. If you need one GPU, a fast CPU box and a notebook, and want to be running in minutes without Kubernetes, Carolina Cloud is the simpler and better-fitting choice.

CoreWeave is an AI hyperscaler: Kubernetes-native, selling whole 8-GPU nodes of H100, H200, B200 and GB200, often under large contracts. Carolina Cloud is a small, simple cloud for teams that need dedicated CPUs, a GPU or two, notebooks and storage.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Carolina Cloud if…

  • You need a single RTX PRO 6000 at $1.75/hr, not an 8-GPU node
  • You want a VM or notebook in seconds without Kubernetes
  • You need dedicated CPU instances at a low hourly price
  • You're a small team, lab or startup

Choose CoreWeave if…

  • You need hundreds or thousands of GPUs, InfiniBand, or GB200 NVL72
  • You have an AI-lab or enterprise-scale training workload and a contract to match

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent a single GPU on CoreWeave?

CoreWeave mostly sells whole multi-GPU nodes. Carolina Cloud lets you attach a single RTX PRO GPU to an instance.

Do both offer free egress?

Yes, both CoreWeave and Carolina Cloud list egress as free.
