Carolina Cloud vs Lambda

Should you use Carolina Cloud or Lambda?

If you're training large models on H100s or B200s, use Lambda. That's what it's built for, and we don't offer those GPUs. If your work mixes CPU-heavy steps with single-GPU inference, fine-tuning, rendering or analysis, Carolina Cloud gives you both in one place, with free egress.

Lambda is a GPU cloud for AI training, with H100 and B200 instances and InfiniBand clusters. Carolina Cloud is a general compute cloud with RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell and RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell GPUs alongside dedicated-core CPU instances, notebooks and S3-compatible storage.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Carolina Cloud if…

  • Your pipeline mixes CPU-heavy steps with GPU steps and you want both in one place
  • Your models fit on a single workstation-class Blackwell GPU
  • You want dedicated CPU instances at a low price next to your GPUs
  • You want JupyterLab or RStudio with a GPU in seconds

Choose Lambda if…

  • You're training large models that need H100s, B200s, or multi-node InfiniBand
  • You need 8-GPU nodes or reserved clusters

Frequently asked questions

Does Carolina Cloud have H100s?

No. Carolina Cloud offers RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell and RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell GPUs. For H100 or B200 training, Lambda is a better fit.

Does Lambda offer CPU-only instances?

Lambda focuses on GPU instances. Carolina Cloud offers dedicated CPU instances from $0.015 per vCPU-hour (with 2 GiB RAM).
