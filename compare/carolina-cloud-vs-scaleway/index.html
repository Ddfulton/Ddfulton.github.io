Carolina Cloud vs Scaleway

Scaleway is a French cloud with a strong European sovereignty story, a wide instance range and GPUs. Carolina Cloud is US-based, focused on dedicated-core compute, GPUs and research environments.

Short answer

If your data must stay in the EU, choose Scaleway. If you're US-based or simply want lower-cost dedicated compute with unmetered egress, Carolina Cloud is cheaper and closer to North American users.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Carolina Cloud if…

  • You're in North America and want low latency
  • You want dedicated compute at a lower hourly price
  • You want RStudio, JupyterLab, genomics tooling and Nextflow ready to go
  • You want egress on storage to be free too

Choose Scaleway if…

  • You need EU data residency or a European provider for sovereignty reasons
  • You want managed Kubernetes, databases or serverless
  • You need H100-class GPUs in Europe

Price comparison

Scaleway's POP2-HC-8C-16G is about €0.21 per hour. Carolina Cloud's 8 vCPU / 16 GB is $0.12–$0.16 per hour. The bigger question is usually location: Scaleway is European, Carolina Cloud is in North Carolina.
