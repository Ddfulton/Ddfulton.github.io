Carolina Cloud vs DNAnexus

Should you use Carolina Cloud or DNAnexus for genomics?

If you need FedRAMP, GxP and 21 CFR Part 11 validation, or a biobank-scale trusted research environment, DNAnexus is the established choice. If you're a startup, lab or core facility that wants to run Nextflow pipelines and notebooks without an enterprise license, Carolina Cloud has no platform fee, published prices and $0 egress.

DNAnexus is an enterprise trusted research environment used by pharma, biobanks such as the UK Biobank Research Analysis Platform, and clinical labs. Carolina Cloud is a cloud for running genomics pipelines, notebooks and analyses with published prices and no platform fee.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Carolina Cloud if…

  • You don't need an enterprise license, just fast pipelines, notebooks and storage
  • You want published prices you can budget against before signing anything
  • You download or share a lot of data and don't want egress charges
  • You want to talk directly to engineers who run Nextflow themselves

Choose DNAnexus if…

  • You need FedRAMP, GxP or 21 CFR Part 11 validated workflows
  • You work with UK Biobank or another biobank hosted on DNAnexus
  • You need WDL or CWL as first-class workflow languages
  • You need an enterprise trusted research environment with validation services

Frequently asked questions

How is DNAnexus priced?

DNAnexus doesn't publish list prices. It charges a license fee plus usage under a negotiated contract. Carolina Cloud publishes its rate card and charges no platform fee.

Does Carolina Cloud support WDL?

Our managed integration is for Nextflow (nf-ccloud). You can run Cromwell or miniwdl on Carolina Cloud VMs, but there's no dedicated WDL integration.

Does Carolina Cloud charge egress?

No. Egress is $0.
