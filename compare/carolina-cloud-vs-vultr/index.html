Carolina Cloud vs Vultr

Should you use Carolina Cloud or Vultr?

Carolina Cloud's dedicated-core instances cost less than Vultr's Optimized Cloud Compute and have unmetered egress. If you need servers close to users on several continents, or datacenter GPUs like H100s and B200s, Vultr is the better choice.

Vultr offers a lot of locations, simple VMs, bare metal and a broad GPU lineup. Carolina Cloud is one US datacenter, built around dedicated-core compute, GPUs and research environments at a lower price.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Carolina Cloud if…

  • You want dedicated cores for less than Vultr's Optimized Cloud
  • You run batch, research or data work and want egress to be free
  • You want ready-made RStudio, JupyterLab and genomics environments
  • You want to talk to an engineer, not a queue

Choose Vultr if…

  • You need locations across many countries
  • You need H100, B200, or other datacenter-class GPUs
  • You want managed Kubernetes or databases
  • You need a cheap shared VPS

Price comparison

Vultr's CPU-optimized 8 vCPU / 16 GB plan is $160 per month. Carolina Cloud's equivalent is about $88 per month general-purpose or $117 high-performance, billed by the hour.

Frequently asked questions

Is Carolina Cloud cheaper than Vultr?

For dedicated-core instances, yes: about $88–$117/mo for 8 vCPU / 16 GB versus $160/mo on Vultr Optimized Cloud.

Does Vultr have more locations?

Yes, by a lot. Vultr has 30+ locations; Carolina Cloud has one datacenter in North Carolina.

Which has better GPUs?

Vultr offers datacenter GPUs such as H100 and B200. Carolina Cloud offers RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell and RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell, which suit inference, fine-tuning, rendering and single-node ML.
